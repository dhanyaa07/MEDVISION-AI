# Known Gaps and Future Validation Work

The current repository does not establish patient-level validation, external-site validation, calibrated confidence, clinical utility, or prospective clinical performance.

Potential research improvements include:
- Patient-level dataset partitioning where patient identifiers and permissions are available.
- External validation on a separately sourced and authorized dataset.
- Probability calibration and calibration evaluation.
- Robustness testing across scanners, acquisition protocols, and image quality.
- Independent review of explainability outputs.
- Reproducible experiment tracking with environment and dataset version metadata.

These are future validation tasks, not capabilities claimed by the current implementation.
