# Limitations and intended use

MEDVISION AI is an academic research prototype.

- Not clinically validated; not for diagnosis or treatment.
- Patient-level independent validation is not established.
- Softmax outputs are not calibrated probabilities.
- Grad-CAM is an approximate model explanation, not tumour segmentation.
- Image-quality statistics do not measure tumour properties.
- The classifier cannot determine tumour size, volume, grade, cause, anatomical extent, or prognosis.
- A `notumor` prediction does not rule out all abnormalities or establish overall brain health.
- Dataset/model redistribution permissions must be checked before publication.
